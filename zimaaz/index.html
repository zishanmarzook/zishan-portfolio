A little about us

Weaving fashion dreams & baby joys.

ZIMAAZ Thread Tales brings together artful embroidery and a love for textile details. Since 2020, maker Mufeena Marzook has been creating pieces for little ones from Muttam, Kannur.

Each design begins with a thoughtful choice of fabric, colour, and detail. Browse the collection, then get in touch to ask about the designs currently available.
